abstract: Community detection algorithms are able to mine and analyze community structures,
  which plays an important part in practical applications. However, for the purpose
  of privacy protection, some communities do not want to be mined and analyzed. For
  this reason, researchers introduced the community deception, which aims to destroy
  the effectiveness of community detection algorithms for the purpose of privacy protection.
  In this paper, we propose an Elite Population Co-evolutionary Genetic (EPCG) algorithm
  for global community deception, in which we design two different fitness functions
  based on Normalized Mutual Information (NMI) and elite population coevolution mechanisms
  respectively. Extensive experiments are conducted on four real networks against
  three classical community detection algorithms. The results validate that our method
  is outperforming the baseline method.
