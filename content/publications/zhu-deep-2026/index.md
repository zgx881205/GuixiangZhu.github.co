abstract: With the rapid evolution of sixth-generation (6G) mobile communications,
  urban air mobility (UAM) networks have emerged as a key pillar to achieve the vision
  of ubiquitous space-air-ground connectivity. Although the terahertz (THz) frequency
  band is regarded as a critical technological enabler for supporting real-time, high-bandwidth
  UAM services due to its abundant spectrum resources, THz waves suffer severe propagation
  attenuation and are highly sensitive to dynamic fluctuations in atmospheric conditions.
  In this paper, we propose an intelligent THz communication system model tailored
  for 6G UAM networks. By incorporating a data-driven machine learning (ML) framework,
  the proposed model enhances the system's adaptability to dynamic environments through
  the deep integration of physical propagation priors with intelligent algorithms.
  Based on the established system model, a deep learning-driven channel prediction
  and ultra-reliable low-latency communications (URLLC) assurance mechanism is introduced.
  Using the powerful nonlinear feature extraction capabilities of deep neural networks
  (DNNs), the system can identify latent atmospheric fading patterns from complex
  THz propagation paths, thereby providing proactive channel state information (CSI)
  for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) nodes. This predictive approach effectively mitigates
  the latency bottlenecks inherent in traditional reactive methods when dealing with
  rapidly changing spatiotemporal features. Extensive numerical simulations demonstrate
  that the proposed intelligent model significantly outperforms traditional benchmark
  schemes in terms of prediction accuracy and system throughput under extreme attenuation
  and highdynamic scenarios. Finally, the results confirm the significant advantages
  of this scheme in maintaining ultra-low end-to-end response latency, providing a
  robust solution for optimizing THz communication performance in complex maritime
  or urban environments.
